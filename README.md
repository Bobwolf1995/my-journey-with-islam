# 🕌 My Journey with Islam

> A modern educational and guidance platform designed to support new Muslims and anyone interested in learning the foundations of Islam through a structured, safe, and interactive learning experience.

## 📖 About the Project

**My Journey with Islam** is an educational Islamic application created to guide users step by step through their learning journey.

The platform combines structured Islamic education, progress tracking, digital resources, community interaction, mentor support, and a carefully controlled AI assistant within one modern application.

The main goal is simple:

> **To make learning Islam clear, organized, accessible, and supportive from the very first step.**

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

The application aims to:

* Provide structured Islamic education for new Muslims.
* Make essential Islamic knowledge easy to understand.
* Guide users through a clear learning path.
* Track learning progress and achievements.
* Provide access to trusted mentors.
* Build a supportive Islamic community.
* Offer a rich digital Islamic library.
* Use AI responsibly without replacing qualified scholars.
* Provide administrators with full control over educational content.

# ✨ Core Features

## 🎓 Learning Courses

The **My Courses** section is completely free and contains structured educational programs covering topics such as:

* Pillars of Islam
* Pillars of Faith
* Prayer
* Purification
* Fasting
* Zakat
* Hajj
* Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
* Islamic Ethics
* Quran Reading Basics

Each course contains organized lessons.

Every lesson may include:

* Detailed explanation
* Key concepts
* Important information
* Common mistakes
* Lesson summary
* Interactive quiz

A lesson is considered completed after successfully passing its quiz.

The default passing score can be configured, for example:

`70%`

## 🤖 Responsible AI Assistant

The application includes an intelligent assistant designed to:

* Answer general educational questions.
* Help users navigate the application.
* Recommend relevant lessons.
* Explain basic concepts.
* Help users find suitable resources.

### AI Safety Principle

The AI assistant **does not issue religious rulings or sensitive Fatwas**.

Questions involving topics such as:

* Halal and Haram rulings
* Divorce
* Marriage rulings
* Inheritance
* Complex religious rulings
* Sensitive personal cases

are redirected to a qualified mentor or specialist.

The AI is designed as an **educational assistant — not a replacement for scholars.**

# 🔐 Safety & Trust

Because the application deals with religious education and potentially sensitive personal questions, the project follows several important principles:

* AI does not replace qualified scholars.
* Sensitive questions are escalated to human mentors.
* Educational content can be reviewed by trusted specialists.
* User privacy is treated as a priority.
* Community content can be moderated.
* Administrative permissions are separated by roles.

# 💡 Why This Project?

Learning Islam can sometimes feel overwhelming for someone who is just beginning.

Information is often scattered across websites, videos, books, social media, and different teachers.

**My Journey with Islam aims to solve this problem by creating one structured learning journey.**

Instead of asking:

> "Where should I start?"

The application provides a clear answer:

> **Start here — and we will guide you to the next step.**

---

# ❤️ Project Philosophy

Technology should make Islamic knowledge easier to access without compromising accuracy, trust, or human guidance.

**My Journey with Islam is designed to be a calm companion throughout the user's journey — from the first question to building strong knowledge and consistent daily practice.**
